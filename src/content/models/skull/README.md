# Skull — Blender source

Low-poly cartoon skull built from `skull-design.jpeg` in this folder (front /
side / top reference sheet). Generated by script, not hand-modelled:

```
./tmp/blender-4.5.15-linux-x64/blender -b -P src/content/models/skull/skull.py [-- --no-render]
```

Outputs: `skull.blend` (editable scene), `skull.glb` (Y-up, materials baked),
`previews/{front,side,top,hero}.png`.

Front = −Y in the source; the Y-up export faces +Z like the pumpkin, so a
runtime swap needs no rotation. Base colours only — no emission. Boot fetches it
and normalises it to `size` (1.0); `../README.md` has the flatten. Cut pieces are
the default scaled clone — the skull has no authored halves.

## Shape rules

- Cranium is **solid**. Two eye sockets and a nose are boolean-intruded pockets
  with dark floors — no inner shell, and no mouth pocket: the grin is the four
  teeth against the jaw.
- Each cutter is a straight prism along +Y with three rings: far outside, the
  authored opening (surface − 0.005) and a floor inset 14 % (eyes) / 10 % (nose)
  toward the path's centroid, riding the shell at its own depth.
- Caps are ear-clipped (`triangulate_2d`). Cutter vertices must stay **off the
  body's x = 0 column**: one on the mirror seam yields 3 non-manifold edges and 3
  degenerate faces. The nose apex and notch are therefore ±0.020 / ±0.006 pairs.
- Materials are assigned per face on the cutter before the boolean and carried
  through by pointer: floor → `SkullSocket`, wall/bevel → `SkullCavity`,
  shell → `SkullBone`. Never reclassify by geometry after the cut.
- The four teeth are separate prisms hung off the jaw — no boolean. Top rings are
  buried above the gum line, the front leans out as it drops, and the back ring
  extrudes along the surface normal so each tooth embeds into the bone. Flanks
  facing a crease use `SkullCavity`; outer flanks stay bone.
- Body is 1.88 wide × 1.79 deep × 1.59 tall; asymmetric `rz_top`/`rz_bot`, lower
  face pushed forward into a plane, brow ridge above the sockets.
- Body splits at 38°, teeth at 46°. Teeth rims stay sharp.

## Tuning

`BODY` (radii, profile, taper, brow, face push, jaw, bottom shift), `COLOURS`,
`EYE` / `EYE_DEPTH` / `EYE_INSET`, `NOSE_DEPTH` / `NOSE_INSET`, `TOOTH` (width,
z span, proud, set, depth, flare), and the `*_path()` outlines: front-plane
(x, z) metres, eye origin at its centre, nose and teeth + x = inner.

## Budget

998 tris — body 870, teeth 128 (4 × 32); `skull.glb` is 29 028 B, inside
`ASSET_BUDGET` (1 100 tris / 34 000 B). Drawn at ~1/14 of the viewport height
(≈60 px), so detail is budgeted for that: 32 × 10 body, 15-point eyes, 16-point
nose, 9-point teeth. Path point count is what costs: every outline vertex adds a
wall quad on both ring joins. Both meshes must stay manifold (0 non-manifold
edges, 0 degenerate faces, bmesh pass); the boolean drops out of EXACT the moment
a cutter path self-touches.
