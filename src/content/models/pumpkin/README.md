# Pumpkin — Blender source

Low-poly jack-o'-lantern built from `src/content/models/pumpkin-design.jpeg`
(front / side / top reference sheet). Generated by script, not hand-modelled:

```
./tmp/blender-4.5.15-linux-x64/blender -b -P src/content/models/pumpkin/pumpkin.py [-- --no-render]
```

Blender is not installed in this repo; the portable 4.5 LTS build lives in the
gitignored `tmp/`. Any 4.5+ `blender` binary works.

Outputs: `pumpkin.blend` (editable scene), `pumpkin.glb` (Y-up, materials baked),
`previews/{front,side,top,hero}.png`.

The game loads `pumpkin.glb` once at boot, merges its primitives into one
vertex-coloured geometry and normalises it to `size` (1.0). Base colours carry
the shell, flesh, glow and stem; emission does not — the glow face is a bright
vertex colour, not a light.

## Shape rules

- The body is **solid**. The face is four boolean-intruded pockets (two eyes,
  nose, mouth) — no inner shell and no cavity to pay for.
- Each cutter is a straight prism along +Y with three rings: far outside, the
  authored opening (surface − 0.005) and a floor inset 4 % toward the path's
  centroid, riding the shell at +0.075. The walls read as a bevel.
- Caps are ear-clipped (`triangulate_2d`). A centroid fan folds over concave
  paths like the grin and produces non-manifold boolean output.
- Materials are assigned per face on the cutter before the boolean and carried
  through by pointer: floor → `PumpkinGlow`, wall/bevel → `PumpkinFlesh`,
  shell → `PumpkinShell`. Never reclassify by geometry after the cut.
- Front is −Y, up is +Z. Body is 2.16 wide × 1.64 tall; 2.05 with the stem.

## Tuning

`BODY` (profile, belly, lobes, groove), `COLOURS`, `POCKET_DEPTH`, and the three
`*_path()` outlines: front-plane (x, z) metres, eye origin on its lower edge,
+x = inner.

## Budget

1 304 tris — body 1 046, stem 92, tendril 166. Path point count is what costs:
every outline vertex adds a wall quad on both ring joins. Body must stay
manifold (0 non-manifold edges, checked with a bmesh pass); the boolean drops
out of EXACT the moment a cutter path self-touches.
